Privacy Policy

Sudoku: Minimal Puzzle · Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information the app “Sudoku: Minimal Puzzle” (package com.romulocotodurante.sudoku) handles. The app is published by Romulo Coto Durante (“I”, “me”).

Information I collect

I do not collect personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of mine that receive your data.

Your game progress, settings and statistics are stored only on your device, in a local database. I cannot see them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Ads

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. This is handled by Google, not by me.

  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.

Remove ads purchase

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase to remove ads. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. I do not receive your payment details; I only receive confirmation that the purchase was made, so the app can turn ads off.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data sharing and retention

I do not sell or share personal data, because I do not collect any. Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
